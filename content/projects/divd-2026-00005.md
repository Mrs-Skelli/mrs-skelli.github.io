DIVD CSIRT research into unintended data exposure in Salesforce Experience Cloud — portals, communities, and custom Aura or Lightning apps where authorization is misconfigured.

The issue is not a vulnerability in Salesforce itself. The platform provides object permissions, field-level security, sharing rules, and guest user restrictions. When those controls are wrong, unauthenticated or low-privileged users can pull data through publicly reachable endpoints such as `/s/sfsites/aura` without an exploit — just direct API interaction.

Typical causes include overly permissive guest users, missing field-level security, broad sharing rules, and Apex or Aura logic that skips server-side authorization. During assessments, exposed data has included personal information, customer records, support cases, and internal documents.

DIVD is scanning publicly accessible Experience Cloud deployments, notifying affected organizations, and publishing guidance on reviewing guest access, sharing rules, and Aura-enabled methods.
